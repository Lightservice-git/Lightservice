Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before you book?

Here are the essentials. If you need something more specific, message us directly.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?
Yes. Pickup and delivery are part of the service. Send your pickup location when booking so we can confirm coverage and arrangements.
How do I book a laundry pickup?
Fill out the booking form on this page. Your details will be prepared as a WhatsApp message for you to review and send.
How much does laundry cost?
Pricing depends on the garment and service. Use the pricing section or WhatsApp us for the current rate for your order.
Do you handle Agbada and special garments?
Yes. Agbada and special wear are listed among our services. Tell us about the garment and any care instructions when you book.
How long will my order take?
Turnaround depends on the service and garment. We recommend asking for the expected turnaround when you place your order, especially for urgent items.
What are the terms of the ₦5,000 monthly subscription?
The website advertises ₦5,000 for ₦10,000 in stated laundry value. Because the eligibility and validity details are not specified here, please confirm the current terms with Light Service before paying.
Tip: For stains, delicate fabrics, valuable garments or special finishing requests, describe the item clearly in the booking notes so the team can advise you before processing.
